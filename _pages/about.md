I am an MSc student in **Robotics, Systems and Control at ETH Zurich**. My interests lie in **embodied AI and 3D vision**, particularly how robots perceive their surroundings and use visual information for navigation and manipulation.

My recent projects span 3D reconstruction from 360° walkthroughs and reinforcement learning for dexterous manipulation.

I received my **B.Eng. in Information Engineering from Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)** in July 2025.
