RIGOR (Rig-Informed Geometry for Omnidirectional Reconstruction) builds 3D maps from 360° construction-site walkthroughs using a frozen feed-forward model and four-view virtual camera rigs. The pipeline combines validated local pose repair, multi-view loop-closure verification and Sim(3) pose-graph optimization before point-cloud fusion.
