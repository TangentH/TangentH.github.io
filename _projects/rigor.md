RIGOR (Rig-Informed Geometry for Omnidirectional Reconstruction) builds 3D maps from 360° construction-site walkthroughs using a frozen feed-forward model and four-view virtual camera rigs. The work focuses on virtual-rig-based local pose repair and multi-view geometric verification for loop closure before point-cloud fusion.
